I · Signal 0.0 – 9.6 s

A dot, then a sweep, then noise. A sine wave locks, and the scope reads 164.81 Hz, the E3 you are hearing.

The dot opens into a raster and a terminal asks one question: > why?

We dive into the dot of the question mark. It is one phosphor pixel made of three scanlines, and one of those scanlines becomes a network link.

II · System 9.6 – 14.4 s

Enter lands on the downbeat of tcpdump -i any. The scanline opens into SYSTEMS, and the letters are windows onto live traffic. One host calls home every 600 ms, exactly one beat of the song.

The pattern is caught. Irrelevant paths fall away, the host's process tree opens to updater 4117, a decision forms, and the host goes dark. A red line stays behind.

III · Curiosity 14.4 – 19.2 s

Pull back and the network is a speck in a sky of nodes. Stars ignite on the bells and fall into line as a light cone.

The shutter fires. The sky becomes a 1986 glass-plate negative, the red line becomes a worldline, and it forks into two worlds.

The plate's measuring grid bends into an Einstein–Rosen bridge, and we dive through the throat.

IV · Human 19.2 – 24.0 s

An amber monitor plots a measurement. The playhead shows it is the music you are hearing. It is a guitar.

Look closer and the trace becomes a red thread on cork, bending with each pluck. Then one beat of silence.

V · Synthesis 24.0 – 30.0 s

The release throws the camera off the thread. It is pinned to a board, and every clue on it is a printed frame of this film, in the order you saw them.

The beam from the first frame runs the thread through every clue, then weaves the answer to why? across the nails.

One thought 30.0 – 34.4 s

Silence. The picture then shuts off like an old CRT and collapses into the dot the film began with, so it loops.
